# "Success Rate" Content & Growth Strategy (90-Day Blueprint)

**Codename**: `Success Rate`  
**Portal**: JobUpdate (India Government Jobs, Admit Cards, Results, State & Central Alerts)  
**Target**: Scale organic search impressions, Google Discover visibility, and achieve 100K+ monthly visitors.

---

## 📅 Weekly Content Routine ("Success Rate" Calendar)

Whenever the user runs scrapers, logs in, or asks what to do, check the current day of the week and recommend the day's designated focus:

| Day of Week | Designated Focus | Content Type & Details | Target Intent / Channel |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Monday** | **Mega Exam Syllabus & Exam Pattern** | In-depth pillar breakdown (e.g., RRB NTPC, SSC CGL/CHSL, RPF SI/Constable, UPSC CSE Prelims, IBPS PO) with subject-wise marks weightage & official PDF download link. | Google Search & Evergreen SEO |
| **Tuesday** | **State-Specific Recruitment Hub** | High-intent state vacancy guide (Focus: Maharashtra, UP, Bihar, Rajasthan, MP) e.g., Police Bharti, Talathi, Patwari, High Court Clerk. | State Job Directory & Regional Search |
| **Wednesday** | **Salary & Job Profile Teardown** | Post-wise in-hand salary slip breakdown, allowances, pay matrix level, and promotion ladder (e.g., Station Master salary, SSC CGL Inspector perks). | Google Discover & Viral Social |
| **Thursday** | **Admit Card & Exam Date Tracker** | Rapid-response updates for upcoming exams, direct hall ticket download links, exam city slip status, and checklist. | Google Search Position Zero & Fast Snippets |
| **Friday** | **Preparation Strategy & Books List** | 60-day / 90-day actionable study plan, top recommended books, topic-wise practice strategy, and time-management tips. | Candidate Bookmark & High Retention |
| **Saturday** | **Answer Key & Expected Cut-Off** | Post-exam review: good attempts, shift-wise difficulty level analysis, past 3-year cutoff comparisons (UR/OBC/SC/ST/EWS). | Post-Exam Search Spikes |
| **Sunday / Daily** | **Automated Scraping & Alert Push** | Run `node scripts/run-all-scrapers.js`, verify latest updates, push to GitHub / Cloudflare Pages, and share top alerts on WhatsApp/Telegram. | Automated Operations & Owned Community |

## 🗓️ 3-Month Strategic Phases

### Month 1: Foundation & Discover Optimization
- Publish 3–4 deep pillar syllabus and exam pattern teardowns per week.
- Optimize high-res Discover-friendly thumbnails (1200×675) with bold bilingual badges.
- Launch WhatsApp Channel & Telegram push alerts for instant mobile subscriber capture.

### Month 2: State Micro-Hubs & High-Intent Utility Tools
- Expand top state portals: Maharashtra, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Rajasthan, MP.
- Promote high-retention utility tools: Age Calculator, Photo/Signature Resizer (SSC/UPSC specs), Qualification Eligibility matcher.
- Deep cross-linking between scraped state jobs and educational syllabus guides.

### Month 3: Post-Exam Cutoff & Evergreen Authority
- Real-time exam shift reviews, answer key tracking, and expected cutoff analysis within 2–4 hours of exam completion.
- In-depth salary slip and lifestyle/perks teardowns for popular posts.
- Targeted seeding on forums (Quora, Reddit r/Indian_Academia).

---

## ✍️ Editorial Post Quality Standard (Every Article Must Have)
1. **Clear, Direct Answer Box**: Position Zero snippet answering eligibility, age, or date in 2 concise sentences.
2. **Structured Tables**: Clean tables for vacancies, salary breakdown, and syllabus topics.
3. **Official Links Box**: Direct official PDF notification, official portal link, and application URL.
4. **FAQ Section**: 4–5 questions with `FAQPage` schema.
5. **Cross-Links**: Direct links to active scraped jobs from the same commission/state.

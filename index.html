Explore All 9 Government Job Recruitment Sections

Sarkari Aavedan categorizes notifications into 9 distinct layout columns so you can find what you need instantly:

  • Latest Jobs (नवीनतम नौकरियां): View all current online application forms for UPSC, SSC, Railways, Bank PO/Clerk, State level recruitments, and police jobs.
  • Admit Card (प्रवेश पत्र): Download official hall tickets and call letters for upcoming competitive exams.
  • Result (परीक्षा परिणाम): Check final selections, written exam merit lists, and scorecards directly.
  • Answer Key (उत्तर कुंजी): Match your responses with official provisional and final keys to evaluate performance.
  • Syllabus (पाठ्यक्रम): Read detailed section-wise exam patterns, syllabus PDFs, and marking schemes.
  • Admission (प्रवेश): Apply for national and state-level entrance exams (like CUET, B.Ed, NVS, ITI) online.
  • Certificate Verification (प्रमाण पत्र सत्यापन): Access details for document verification, UP scholarship status, and NIELIT CCC exam applications.
  • Outsourcing / Offline Jobs: Discover contracts, contractual teacher vacancies (KGBV), state transport conductors (UPSRTC), and offline forms.
  • Important Announcements (महत्वपूर्ण घोषणाएं): Access essential notices, voter ID status, self-enumeration instructions, and scholarship portals.

Bilingual Job Specifications & Detailed Vacancy Breakdowns

Unlike other portals, every job specification on Sarkari Aavedan features a print-friendly summary card that contains important dates (application deadlines, exam dates), category-wise application fee groups, age criteria with relaxation parameters, and category-wise post distributions.

All applicant links are automatically unwrapped to target official authority servers directly. Candidates can apply online, download notifications, or check official websites (like upsc.gov.in, ssc.gov.in, or rrbcdg.gov.in) with a single click.

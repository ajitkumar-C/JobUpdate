How to Get Maharashtra Government Jobs 2026 — Exams, Eligibility & Process

To get a महाराष्ट्र सरकारी नोकरी (Maharashtra government job), candidates typically appear for MPSC exams (State Service), SLPRB (Police), NHM (Health), ZP (Zilla Parishad), or central exams (SSC/IBPS/RRB). Choose the right exam based on your qualification and check mh.indgovtjobs.net daily for the latest भरती जाहिराती (recruitment notifications).

MPSC Recruitment 2026 — Group 1, Group 2, Group 3 (एमपीएससी भरती)

MPSC Group 1 notification 2026 covers 100+ Class A/B posts including Deputy Collector, DSP, and Tahsildar. MPSC Group 2 includes 800+ posts such as State Tax Inspector, PSI, and ASO. The MPSC Group 1 mains syllabus 2026 follows: Prelims (GS + CSAT) → Mains Paper 1 (Marathi/English qualifying) + Papers 2–4 (General Studies) + Paper 5 (Optional, 18 subjects). Full syllabus PDF available at mpsc.gov.in.

Maharashtra Police Bharti 2026 — Constable, PSI, SRPF Recruitment

Maharashtra Police constable bharti 2026 releases thousands of vacancies via SLPRB. Selection process: Written Exam → PMT (Physical Measurement) → PET (Physical Efficiency) → Medical Examination. Age limit: General 18–28 years | SC/ST +5 | OBC/VJNT +3. View Maharashtra Police Bharti notifications →

NHM Maharashtra Recruitment 2026 — Health Jobs (आरोग्य नोकऱ्या)

NHM Maharashtra recruitment 2026 (National Health Mission) offers posts for Staff Nurse, ANM, Community Health Officer (CHO), Lab Technician, ASHA Facilitator, and Medical Officer across all 36 districts. Apply online at nhmmaharashtra.gov.in. View NHM and Health Jobs →

Frequently Asked Questions — Maharashtra Sarkari Naukri 2026

How to get a government job in Maharashtra?

To get a महाराष्ट्र सरकारी नोकरी, appear for MPSC (State Service), SLPRB (Police), NHM, ZP Bharti, SSC, IBPS, or RRB exams. Bookmark mh.indgovtjobs.net for daily recruitment notifications — all free.

When will MPSC Group 1 notification 2026 be released?

The MPSC Group 1 2026 notification will be published on mpsc.gov.in. It is expected to include 100+ Class A/B posts such as Deputy Collector, DSP, and Tahsildar. Bookmark mh.indgovtjobs.net for instant updates when it drops.

What is the age limit for Maharashtra Police constable 2026?

General category: 18–28 years | SC/ST: +5 years relaxation | OBC/VJNT/NT: +3 years relaxation. Selection process: Written Exam → PMT (Physical Measurement) → PET (Physical Efficiency) → Medical Examination.

Which government jobs are available for 10th pass in Maharashtra?

10th pass (दहावी) candidates in Maharashtra can apply for Anganwadi Helper (WCD), Police Constable (SLPRB), Group D posts, Peon, and ZP/Gram Panchayat contractual posts. Check eligibility and apply at mh.indgovtjobs.net.

How to apply for ZP Maharashtra Bharti 2026?

Apply online at mahazp.in or the respective district ZP website for Teacher, Health Worker, and Clerk posts. Reservations for SC/ST/OBC/EWS/Divyang apply as per Maharashtra government rules.
